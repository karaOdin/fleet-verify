## The rules

- `off_route`: more than 150 m from the line for more than 10 minutes in a row, outside the line's known places.
- `idle_outside_terminal`: under 3 km/h for more than 30 minutes in a row, outside the line's known places, on the route or off it.
- `silent`: no position for more than 10 minutes from a device that moved on its line in the 30 minutes before, whose last
  position was not at a known place and was not a standstill off the line (trackers report only every ~15 min with the engine off).
- Known places of a line: its two terminal zones (300 m) and its layover places. Nothing counts before the bus first reaches
  its line, nor at its overnight parking place.

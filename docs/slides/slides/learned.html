WHAT THE DATA TAUGHT US

The paperwork is a habit

Times by template

Written times often follow a “+15 minutes” pattern, not the clock.

Aller or retour?

Only 52–65 % of written directions match the GPS. One paper has every direction backwards.

Devices move

A device registered on one bus drives another, sometimes named after a bus in another city.

Rows repeat

1.2 % of GPS rows arrive twice. The results are the same with or without them.

A result is a recommendation for a person, never a sanction (law 18-07).

43–47 min. Direction is a note, never a verdict: papers write aller and retour inconsistently. The decision is always a person’s.

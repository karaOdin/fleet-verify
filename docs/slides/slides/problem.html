THE PROBLEM

A paper written by hand, a GPS that never stops

668

declared trips

on 106 road papers: trip number, aller or retour, departure, arrival, tickets. Written by the receivers.

290,937

GPS positions

from the buses’ trackers in one day: one fix every 10 to 20 seconds per device.

3

questions

Did the trip happen? When did it really leave and arrive? In which direction?

The paper is the company’s record of work and money (tickets). The GPS is what the bus did. We put the two side by side for every trip of the day. On the page: the table "Bus A: written vs GPS".

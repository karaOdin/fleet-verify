KAFKA IN FOUR WORDS

A log you can read again

Topic

fleet.positions: every fix, in arrival order, kept for a week.

Key

The device. One device’s fixes stay in order, on one partition.

Offset

A place in the log. A reader that stops resumes where it left off.

Consumer group

live_trips and lake_sink read the same log, each at its own pace.

Kafka is a log, not a queue: reading does not delete. That is what the pause demo shows: stop live_trips, positions keep arriving, the lag on its node grows; start it again and it catches up without losing anything. Offsets are also why a demo reset deletes the consumer groups: an old offset past the end of a new topic made a reader start over.
